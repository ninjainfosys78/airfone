Most businesses in Nepal still run on the phone. Customers call to ask a price, check stock, book a time or complain. When nobody answers, most of them do not leave a message. They call the next number.

An AI call agent picks up those calls. This guide covers what it does, what it needs from you, and where you should still keep a person on the line.

## What an AI call agent actually does

It answers your existing phone number, speaks with the caller in Nepali, and answers from information you give it: a price list, opening hours, a few documents, notes about common questions.

On a typical call it will:

- greet the caller with your business name
- answer questions about prices, stock, timing and location
- take a name and number, book a slot or hold an item
- pass the call to a person when the caller asks, or when it should

Every call is recorded and written out, so you can read what was asked and how it was answered.

## What it needs from you

The agent knows only what you teach it. The businesses that get good results give it three things in the first week:

1. **A current price list.** A spreadsheet or a PDF is enough.
2. **The ten questions you hear most.** Write the answer you would give.
3. **A rule for handover.** For example: complaints, refunds and anything about a customer's own account go to a person.

When something changes, change it in one place. The next caller hears the new answer.

## Where it helps most

From the calls we have listened to, the biggest gains come in three places:

- **Busy hours.** It answers up to 15 calls at once, so nobody hears a busy tone at the peak.
- **After hours.** Evening and early morning callers get an answer instead of a ringing phone.
- **Repeated questions.** Your staff stop answering "what time do you close" thirty times a day.

## Where a person should still pick up

An agent is not a replacement for your team. Keep a person on calls about money already paid, on complaints and on anything with medical, legal or safety consequences. AirFone hands those calls over mid-call, with a short summary, so the caller does not have to repeat themselves.
