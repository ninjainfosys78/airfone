On salary day, the phone lines at most banks and cooperatives are full. Members want to know the deposit rate, which branch is open on Saturday, or what papers a loan needs. Few of these calls need a person.

## The calls that fill the line

- Fixed deposit and savings rates
- Branch locations and opening hours
- Documents needed for a loan or an account
- How to use mobile banking

These answers already exist in rate sheets and notices. An AI call agent reads them out, in Nepali, to every caller at once.

## What stays with your staff

Anything about a member's own account. The agent does not see balances or transactions. When a caller asks about their account, it passes the call to your team with a note of what was asked.

## Keeping answers current

Rates change monthly. Upload the new rate sheet and the next caller hears the new rate. There is no script to rewrite.

## Recording and review

Every call is recorded and transcribed. Your compliance team can search calls by topic and listen back.
