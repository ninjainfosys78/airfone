A visitor lands on your site with one question. If they cannot find the answer in a minute, they leave. A voice agent lets them simply ask.

## How it works

You add one line of code to your site. A talk button appears. The visitor taps it, allows the microphone, and speaks. The agent answers from the same information your phone agent uses: your pages, price lists and notes.

## What visitors ask

On shop and service sites, the same few questions come up:

- Do you deliver to my city?
- Can I pay on delivery?
- Is this in stock?
- When are you open?

## Turning answers into leads

When a visitor is ready to buy, the agent takes their name and number and sends them to your team with the conversation attached.

## Voice or chat?

Some visitors prefer to type. The [chatbot](/products/website-chatbot) uses the same knowledge, so you can offer both.
